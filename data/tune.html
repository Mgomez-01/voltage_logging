1. Power step test (PID off)

Set up the whole fixture as for a measurement. The heater holds the first power until the sheet settles (at least 2 min, flatter than 0.15 °C/min), then steps to the second power and records the response until it settles again. The heater then turns off. Pick powers that put the sheet around your working range: from the 2026-10-26 run, about 25–35 % holds the sheet near 85–100 °C. Repeat at a second range if the gains differ much. The test stops if the sheet passes 120 °C.

2. Model and suggested gains

Run a power step test. The model is fitted to the step response as it comes in.

3. Setpoint test (PID on)

Runs the PID with these gains through up to 4 setpoints (comma separated), holding each, then turns the heater off. Overshoot, settling time (to ±0.5 °C) and remaining error are measured for each step.

"Apply" changes the running gains (lost at reboot). "Save" also stores them on the board's flash (/pid.json), loaded at every boot. Uploading the filesystem image again erases the saved gains.
